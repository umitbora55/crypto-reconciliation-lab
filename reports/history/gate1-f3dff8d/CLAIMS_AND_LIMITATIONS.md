# Claims and limitations

The available evidence supports finite local correctness and specific failure-handling observations. It does not support general superiority, real institutional deployment, production readiness or a universal reliability percentage. A single overall PASS would conceal the unresolved real-data, remote-CI and Python-3.14 source-suite qualifications.

## Interpretation traps explicitly excluded

- MATCHED is a result under the configured evidence and cut, not proof that all external records exist. Count-only manifests cannot identify an economically equivalent same-count substitution.
- MISMATCH is a discrepancy for investigation, not a cause, loss, insolvency, fraud or regulatory breach.
- REPRODUCED means the current engine reproduces the complete saved answer from two bound inputs. A faithfully reproduced MISMATCH or INCOMPLETE can be REPRODUCED. It is not an independent financial algorithm or original executable verification.
- A valid signature binds bytes to a locally authorized key. It does not prove correct economics, real institution identity, complete custody obligations or compliance.
- Recorded execution's local request identity and durable retry behavior do not establish distributed exactly-once processing.
- Treasury public cash aggregates are not customer custody liabilities. SEC FTD outstanding balances are not daily newly failed trades, individual fail ages or proof of unlawful short sales. Source documentation remains unverified in this task because access failed.
- Zero observed errors, false positives or candidate secret patterns does not imply zero underlying risk. The property bootstrap's degenerate intervals reflect fixed test composition, not certain institutional performance.
- Diagnostic reruns cannot erase the original Python-3.14 source failure. The P2 synchronization issue remains open without a protected-test repair.

## Additional evidence required before institutional conclusions

A legitimate pilot would require explicit data-owner permission, legally reviewable source terms, deidentified or appropriately governed two-sided transaction and custody records, authoritative account/instrument identifiers, revision history, actual receive clocks, completeness guarantees and exact operational cut definitions. It would also require an agreed independent oracle, real exception/adjudication labels, held-out evaluation groups, equivalent competitor semantics and retention/export behavior, controlled deployment resources, and operational failure/recovery exercises appropriate to that institution.

Cost claims additionally need measured institution resource bills, utilization, labor/maintenance assumptions and dated region-specific unit prices. Risk-reduction claims need a defensible causal design or bounded paired intervention, actual incident/loss definitions and observation windows. A local CPU ratio cannot supply either claim.

For MKK, Takasbank or Borsa İstanbul, the presently supportable comparison is workflow similarity: explicit cutoffs, revisions, completeness, independent custody/obligation sources and auditable exceptions. Their actual architecture, product use, relative strength and operational outcomes were not observed. We make neither a use/non-use assertion nor a superiority or accreditation claim.

## Remaining delivery boundaries

Real-data source access and GitHub API access remain external blockers. Local source and installed tests do not replace exact-commit remote CI. macOS arm64, physical storage/power faults, million-event capacity, long soak, natural-error detection, institutional economics and actual policy compliance remain untested or not evaluable. The old frozen ML experiment was not reopened. Any future broadened assertion requires its own registered population, acceptance condition, executed command, raw evidence and explicit uncertainty.
