# S12-F2: original acceptance epochs

`acceptance_epochs` summarizes original admissions of the physical inputs in a
recorded context. It does not summarize every authorization attempt. A later
attempt can be authorized as `CURRENT` and still replay an existing effect.
Filtering only `HISTORICAL_REPLAY` decisions therefore does not implement this
contract.

The writer and saved-context reader now use one bounded, local derivation. For
each captured `auth_delivery_links` row it checks the corresponding physical
`delivery_effects` row, its unique `COMMITTED` attempt, the original attempt's
`auth_attempt_links` decision, and the decision's stream/sequence. The resulting
original decision epochs are deduplicated and sorted. Existing inventory
validation still requires complete protected effect/attempt link coverage. The
helper reads only the already captured tables; it does not inspect current
database authority or verify source signatures.

All replay attempts and their authorization decisions remain in the context.
`observed_policy_head` continues to represent the policy head observed by the
recorded transaction. No financial calculation, policy activation, admission,
cryptography, schema, format, or context claim changes here.

## Five real admission steps

The regression uses the public synthetic fixture, real protected admission and
real policy activation. Expected epoch sets are literal constants. A separate
SQL join checks original delivery/effect/COMMITTED-attempt relationships; it
does not call the production epoch helper.

| Step | Original epochs | Observed head | Physical receipts/effects | Attempts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Initial admitted input | `[1]` | 1 | 30 | 30 |
| Activate epoch 2 | `[1]` | 2 | 30 | 30 |
| Four CURRENT custodian replays | `[1]` | 2 | 30 | 34 |
| 26 historical platform replays | `[1]` | 2 | 30 | 60 |
| New original delivery with rotated key | `[1,2]` | 2 | 31 | 61 |

The final delivery is an economic duplicate with a new physical receipt and
effect. The five financial run IDs and saved financial payloads remain equal.
The original execution is read and retried after all later operations without
recapture, recomputation, changed IDs, or changed durable rows. Legacy unsigned
contexts have an empty acceptance epoch list.
